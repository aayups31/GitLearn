# OutClass identity and homepage

OutClass is the product name selected by the owner on October 10, 2026. The promise is: **Break free from tutorial hell. OutClass the lesson.**

The page uses the spacious typography and product demonstrations of Framer and Devin, and the large visual storytelling of Scale, without copying their branding, assets, or product claims. A broken blue orbit is the signature: a repeated lesson loop opens into a path towards independent work. The palette is ink, cool white, electric blue, and quiet slate. Manrope remains the display face, with Inter for body text and monospace for functional labels.

References inspected: [Framer](https://www.framer.com/), [Devin](https://devin.ai/), [Scale](https://scale.com/), and [GSAP ScrollTrigger documentation](https://gsap.com/docs/v3/Plugins/ScrollTrigger/).

The conversion is a Windows download. The story moves through recognizing tutorial dependency, practicing before the course ends, building with sourced guidance, and investigating a real contribution. The web page stays static and never connects to the user's companion or receives learning data.

GSAP and ScrollTrigger ship from the same origin with their license. Motion includes a hero entrance, a reversible orbit/parallax sequence, a perspective workspace reveal, staggered section entrances, and a progress sequence beside practical-learning steps. No scroll interception or video downloads. Phone layouts use normal document flow. Reduced motion, missing scripts, and keyboard navigation keep the complete story and downloads usable.

The download panel links the tested OutClass 0.4.2 preview. The submitted GitLearn 0.4.1 Chrome item remains under its existing review submission until the next store package update; changing the public site does not create a second extension listing or cancel Google's review. The privacy page identifies OutClass as the new name for GitLearn.

Compatibility identities such as the GitHub repository, app ID, saved-data locations, environment variables, and extension message namespace stay stable. Display names change without relocating a learner's files or invalidating existing pairing.
